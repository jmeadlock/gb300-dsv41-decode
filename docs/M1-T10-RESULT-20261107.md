## V0 row-invariance census (row r at 6 rows vs the same row alone, `torch.equal`)
Row-invariant: DG masked grouped GEMM, RMSNorm (torch), act_quant, cuBLAS bf16 `F.linear`, einsum bf16 (cuBLAS bmm), reference `fp4_gemm`, reference `fp8_gemm`, our GEMV with the M=1 config pinned, sparse attention (ref/bounded/FlashMLA).
**Not** row-invariant: cuBLAS **fp32** `F.linear` (the head: the reference widens to fp32 and cuBLAS picks a different split per M) and our GEMV with the tuned M=6 configs (different split-K order; pinning the M=1 config restores invariance at a measured cost to be read from the census table).

Consequence for the parity instrument: with the M=1 GEMV configs pinned, the only non-invariant kernel is the fp32 head — a 6-row runtime can be made bit-identical to 1-row greedy everywhere except the final logits' last ulp, which is where near-tie argmax flips live. Whether to pin (cost) or drift-grade (A3 class) is a decision for the A3 amendment.

## What R6/R1 = 2.0 means
The memo's cost model (`M1-DSPARK-DESIGN.md` §3) predicted 1.4–2.0× on today's kernels; measured 2.0 at the top of the range, on the *slow* MoE path (dg-masked copies up to 36 experts per layer; in-place removes that). Scaling the fast 1-row path (10.52 ms) by 2.0 gives **~21 ms per 6-row step** before any 6-row-specific work; the memo's "15–24 ms after in-place MoE + residency" bracket stands. Against the headline: 7.36 ms production, 5.0 bar. **M1 at today's kernels lands ~3× off the bar**, as the memo said; the per-position number (8.46 → ~3.5 ms/position at the fast path) is the one that improves tokens/s, and the per-step number is the one the charter grades.

| fused glue at 6 rows | only `swiglu_shared`, `hc_post` run fused; the other 16 clusters run **reference glue** (16+ launches per cluster per layer at 6 rows) |

Reading: the M2 memo (§0.4) predicted this split — a bottom-up 6-row fast path with row-generic glue is 13–15 ms; "if the measurement comes in high, the excess is M1 port work". It came in at 40: the reference glue at 6 rows (plus the 36-pair dedup glue, the GEMV at M=6 with tuned configs — 1-row GEMV pair/fold are guarded `_rows == 1`) costs ~25 ms. So the ratio is **not** a hardware fact: R6/R1 = 2.0 (slow MoE, ref glue) and 3.57 (fast MoE, ref glue) both measure "how much of the step is still unfused at 6 rows". The 6-row port of the 16 fused clusters + the GEMV pair/fold + S=16 is the next coding round (M1-T10-PORT); expected 6-row step ≈ 14–16 ms (memo bottom-up), ≈ 1.4× the 1-row step.
