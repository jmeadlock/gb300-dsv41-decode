# Worker goals (round 2) — each reads CONTEXT.md first

W1 — **The launch boundary itself** (`W1-launch-boundary.md`): the step is ~2,800 graph nodes × ~3.7 µs. Research every way to make a kernel boundary cheaper *without* fusing: CUDA **Programmatic Dependent Launch** (PDL: `cudaLaunchAttributeProgrammaticStreamSerialization` + `cudaGridDependencySynchronize`/`griddepcontrol`) — does it work inside captured CUDA graphs on CUDA 13 (programmatic edges, `cudaGraphKernelNodePortProgrammatic`), what does Triton expose (`launch_pdl`, `tl.extra.cuda.gdc_wait/launch_dependents` — which Triton version; the image's), what do TileLang / CuTe DSL / DeepGEMM / FlashMLA already do (DeepGEMM has PDL support in recent versions — which commit; is it in the vLLM 0.31.0 vendored copy); measured gains reported by vLLM/SGLang/TRT-LLM when they enabled PDL for decode (cite PRs with numbers). Also: graph node count vs replay cost (does cudaGraphLaunch time scale with nodes on Blackwell; graph "device launch" / `cudaGraphInstantiateFlagDeviceLaunch`; conditional nodes), kernel-size floors on sm_100/103 (minimum launch-to-launch gap in a graph for a trivial kernel — any published microbench), and `nvjet`/cuBLAS small-M behaviour vs a handwritten GEMV. Deliver: the ranked list with modeled ms for our 2,800-node step at 1 and 6 rows, numerics class (PDL changes nothing numerically), and the ≤30-min box probe for each (e.g. "same graph with PDL attributes on every Triton launch: measure step cell").

W2 — **DSpark / MTP: production's loop and the token yield** (`W2-dspark-yield.md`): everything public about DeepSeek V4/V4.1-Flash DSpark (tech report, model card, the `n_mtp_layers=3`/`dspark_markov_rank`/confidence head design), vLLM's implementation of the speculative loop for this model (`vllm/model_executor/.../deepseek_v41`, the `dspark.py` draft model and the engine-side proposer/verifier in vLLM 0.31 — where acceptance is decided, greedy vs confidence-gated, how many rows per step, how rejected positions' KV is handled, what the drafter costs per step), SGLang's equivalent, published acceptance lengths for this family (per prompt class), and what production's 7.36 ms step contains (verify + draft + scheduling). Price: at our projected ~21 ms 6-row verify + X ms draft, what tokens/step is realistic, hence ms/token; whether *more* than 5 drafts (k=7, 9: more rows at ~row-invariant kernel cost since the step is latency-bound) raises tokens/step more than it costs; whether the confidence head lets us skip draft steps. Numerics: acceptance = exact greedy match means the emitted stream equals 1-row greedy (bit-identical instrument preserved) — confirm or refute from the vLLM code.

W3 — **GEMV and head: the bandwidth-bound residual** (`W3-gemv-head.md`): our Triton MXFP8 GEMV runs 290 launches/step at 9.6 µs each (~1.7 TB/s on N ≤ 2304 shapes; 5.9 GB of fp8 weights/step would take 0.75 ms at 8 TB/s); the fp32 head is one 420 µs cuBLAS gemv over 2.65 GB (0.33 ms at HBM rate). Research: state-of-the-art decode GEMV/skinny-GEMM kernels for Blackwell (FlashInfer, TRT-LLM `weightOnlyBatchedGemv`/`fp8 gemv`, cuBLASLt small-M fp8, Marlin-class, "stream-K"/persistent split-K, CUTLASS 4.x grouped/ptr-array GEMM for *many small* GEMMs in one launch) — what TB/s they reach at M=1–8 on N×K like 1280×5120, 2304×5120, 5120×8192, 25600×6144, 32768×1280 (cite numbers + GPU + date); whether one **grouped GEMV launch per layer** (all ~7 projections of a layer in one kernel via a pointer array) is a known pattern with numbers; the fp32 head: does anyone keep the vocab projection in fp32 for this model (what does vLLM do for V4.1-Flash's head — bf16? that would be an A1-class deviation for us: say so), and the cost of a bf16 vs fp32 head read. Deliver modeled ms for our 290 + head at 1 and 6 rows per option, numerics class, and box probes.

W4 — **Upstream watch + numerics at M>1** (`W4-upstream-and-rowinvariance.md`): (a) since our pins (vLLM 0.31.0 image; DeepGEMM `88965b07…`; FlashMLA as vendored; Triton/TileLang/cutlass versions in `docs/ENVIRONMENT.md`): merged changes in DeepGEMM (fp8×fp4 grouped GEMM, SF handling, PDL, SM103 tuning), FlashMLA (sparse decode/prefill ops, SM103), vLLM/SGLang DeepSeek-V4.1 paths (fused norm+quant+rope ops like `fused_norm_rope_attn_rope_cast_decode` we saw in the FlashMLA `.so` schema — what does it compute, could it replace our q/kv path kernels bit-exactly?), FlashInfer MoE/GEMV for MXFP4 — each with merged date, tag, pure-Python vs csrc (overlay-testable on our image or not). Deliver: upstream table + the row-invariance options with numerics class and a box probe.
