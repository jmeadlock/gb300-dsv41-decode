Public repo + living blog post at measured milestones.

## My reading / plan
The 6-row step is now bit-identical to the 1-row step and within A3 of production; the remaining 13 ms to 7.36 is launches and per-kernel fixed cost, not bytes (bytes floor at 6 rows ≈ 4.6–6.3 ms). Order I intend: (1) PDL + prefetch on owned kernels (bit-identical, probe says 1–3.5 ms); (2) fold/consolidate rungs that cut node count bit-identically (L6 folds + concat-free, L8 indexer/compressor glue ~230 launches, L9 o-front, L10 once fixed); (3) revisit L1 only if the priority variant shows the contention is schedulable; (4) MegaMoE/option A: no, unless the numerics can be brought back to our oracle distance (e.g., a variant that quantizes the L2 input from bf16); (5) a persistent per-layer kernel (the memo's "stretch", ~7.3 ms modeled) is the only path I see to actually beating 7.36 — everything above lands ~15–17 ms. I may be wrong about (5): tokens/step is 2.9 vs production's ~2.8, so ms/token could beat production's 2.6 only via step time; there is no acceptance lever left (exact-match acceptance, block-5 drafter, k>5 doesn't help — measured in a research round).
